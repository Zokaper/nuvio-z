Nuvio Z iOS Installer for Mac

One universal app for Intel and Apple Silicon (M-series), macOS 11 or later.
Unzip and move Nuvio Z iOS Installer.app to Applications, then open it.

This build is ad-hoc signed, without Apple notarization. If macOS blocks it,
open System Settings > Privacy & Security, choose Open Anyway for this app,
and confirm Open. Only do this for the copy downloaded from Zokaper/nuvio-z.

Connect your iPhone by USB, unlock it and follow the installer's steps.
Apple's iPhone connection software is already included with macOS.
Install sets up Nuvio Z; Repair reinstalls it while keeping its data.
After installation, follow the daily refresh guide on your iPhone.

The new installer installs Nuvio Z directly. Coming from the old SideStore
setup? Follow the release's migration instructions before installing.
